# Org chart

## Leadership (Opus)

- **Mira Vale, Creative Director.** Owns the design bible, art direction, asset sign-off and round triage.
- **Theo Brandt, Engineering Manager.** Owns the task board, branch merges, the build and the release.

## Asset Studio (Haiku swarm)

Wallpaper, icons, glass and gloss materials, orbs, bubbles, cursors. Every asset is drawn
(SVG, or rendered with Python/PIL) or sourced under a CC0/public-domain licence and credited.

## Shell Squad (Haiku swarm)

Window chrome, taskbar, Start orb and menu, desktop icons, clock, global tokens and typography.

## Content Squad (Haiku swarm)

The About, Experience, Projects, Research, Contact and Games windows.

## Games Squad (Haiku swarm)

The seven games' skins (kit.js and each game's palette) so they sit inside the new chrome.

## Review Guild (Haiku swarm)

Every branch gets a peer review before merge.

## QA Guild (Haiku swarm)

Screenshot-driven QA after each integration: shell, windows, games, phone, accessibility,
Frutiger Aero authenticity, asset quality.
